# Judge demo script — 75 seconds

> Run `npm run dev`, open <http://localhost:5180>, and reset state first from
> **Settings → Demonstration controls → Reset demonstration state** (or
> `curl -X POST localhost:5179/api/demo/reset`).

| Time | Do this | What the judge sees |
|------|---------|---------------------|
| 0:00–0:12 | On the Gateway, click **Access Console**. Click it again. | The primary CTA slides away, twice, captioned "Adaptive Affordance Programme: confirming deliberate intent (1 of 2)". Point out that **Continue as guest** has been sitting underneath it the whole time. |
| 0:12–0:22 | Click it a third time. | A three-question **Pre-Authentication Readiness Assessment** opens. Read question 3 aloud: *"Do you consent to your consent being recorded?"* Click **Skip and continue as guest**. |
| 0:22–0:34 | In the Console, type `copilot` into search and press Enter. | "**Zero results, precisely.** 0 records contain the exact character sequence 'copilot'" — Literal Interpretation Policy LIP-7. Click **Search the way a human would**: the record appears. Every joke has an exit. |
| 0:34–0:42 | Set **Status** to `Active`. | 14 of 14 records, with a footnote citing Clause 14(b): every allocation has *held* status "active" at some point. Click **Interpret in plain English instead** → 11 records. |
| 0:42–0:56 | Click the **sun icon** in the header. | A **Luminance Change Request** (Form LCR-1) demanding a 40-character business justification. Fill it, tick the advisory, submit. Watch the four-step "review", then the approval and its **document control number**. The theme really changes and persists. |
| 0:56–1:08 | Go to **Attestations** → **Generate attestation**. | The bar climbs to **99%** and stops: a Secondary Attestor must confirm *in writing* that they are not a robot. Type `I am not a robot` → 100%, certified, written to the database. |
| 1:08–1:15 | Dismiss the toast in the corner two or three times. | The governance notice returns, escalating through five increasingly bureaucratic tiers. At tier 3 it offers **File cessation request**, which genuinely silences it forever. |

**If you have 15 seconds more:** open **Ask ARBITER** and type *"this is infuriating,
can I speak to a human?"* — it will explain that human colleagues are allocated by the
same quota system you are looking at, and that the human quota is at 100% utilisation.

**If you have 15 seconds more than that:** open **Audit trail**. Everything you just
did is there, timestamped. It is the only honest component in the product.
